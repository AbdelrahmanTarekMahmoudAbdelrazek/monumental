The Copy · free prototype

It learns short phrases in your real voice, then answers your questions with them — like the Copy will with a friend's voice. Free, no key.
Headphones recommended. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  1. Hold the button (or Space) and say ONE short answer, then let go: «ايوه سامعك» … «انا هنا» … «جاي» … «اه» … «متخافش انا جنبك». One phrase per press.
  2. Then hold and ask: «سامعني؟», «انت فين؟», «تعالى», «انا خايف». It answers with your own recorded phrase.
Chrome's free speech-to-text sends what you say to Google to turn it into text. The recordings of your voice stay in this tab and are forgotten when you close it.
