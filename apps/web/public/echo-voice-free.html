The Copy · free prototype

It learns short phrases in your real voice, then answers your questions with them — like the Copy will with a friend's voice. Free, no key. "Google" uses Chrome's built-in speech-to-text set to Egyptian Arabic (ar-EG) — the most accurate for Egyptian. "Whisper" runs fully inside your browser (private) but is weaker on Egyptian.
Not started.
  1. Hold the button (or Space), wait until it turns red and says “Speak now”, say ONE short answer, then let go: «ايوه سامعك» · «انا هنا» · «انا في الممر» · «جاي» · «اه» · «لا» · «مش عارف» · «متخافش انا جنبك» · «لا مش خايف».
  2. Hold and ask: «سامعني؟» «انت فين؟» «تعالى» «انت خايف؟» «انت شفت الوحش؟». It answers with your recorded phrase.
  3. No good answer yet? Say the answer in the next press — it remembers it for that question.
The first Start downloads the speech model once (then it's cached). Headphones recommended. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
