The Copy · free prototype

It learns short phrases in your real voice, then answers your questions with them — like the Copy will with a friend's voice. Free, no key. The speech AI (Whisper) runs inside your browser: your voice never leaves this computer.

  1. Hold the button (or Space) and say ONE short answer, then let go: «ايوه سامعك» · «انا هنا» · «انا في الممر» · «جاي» · «اه» · «لا» · «مش عارف» · «متخافش انا جنبك» · «لا مش خايف».
  2. Hold and ask: «سامعني؟» «انت فين؟» «تعالى» «انت خايف؟» «انت شفت الوحش؟». It answers with your recorded phrase.
  3. No good answer yet? Say the answer in the next press — it remembers it for that question.
The first Start downloads the speech model once (then it's cached). Headphones recommended. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
