The Copy · free prototype

It learns short phrases in your real voice, then answers your questions with them. A small meaning AI (runs in your browser) understands what you ask, even new ways of asking — like the Copy will with a friend's voice. Free, no key. "Google" uses Chrome's built-in speech-to-text set to Egyptian Arabic (ar-EG) — the most accurate for Egyptian. "Whisper" runs fully inside your browser (private) but is weaker on Egyptian.

  1. Hold the button (or Space), wait until it turns red and says “Speak now”, say ONE short answer, then let go: «ايوه سامعك» · «انا هنا» · «انا في الممر» · «جاي» · «اه» · «لا» · «مش عارف» · «متخافش انا جنبك» · «لا مش خايف».
  2. Hold and ask: «الو» «انت فين؟» «انت هنا؟» «عامل ايه؟» «الساعه كام؟» «انت خايف؟» «تعالى». Each question only gets an answer that fits it.
  3. No fitting answer yet? It tells you — say the answer in the next press and it remembers it for that kind of question. Wrong answer? Press ✗ wrong answer under it, then say the right one.
The first Start downloads the speech model once (then it's cached). Headphones recommended. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
